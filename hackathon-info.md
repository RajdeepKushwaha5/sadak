The prompts
Pick one. Build it your way.
Three prompts below, each drawn from a real product problem we're solving for learners — or bring your own.

Prompt 01
Math
K–5 Math Game
Develop an interactive, gamified math experience tailored for elementary students that makes foundational arithmetic concepts both intuitive and engaging.

We're looking for: innovative mechanics that encourage steady progression and reward mastery of core numeracy skills.
Prompt 02
Language
Language Learning App
Create a structured, user-friendly mobile experience that simplifies the acquisition of a new language through spaced repetition or immersive daily practice.

We're looking for: a strong grasp of pedagogical design, with a seamless and intuitive user journey.
Prompt 03
Literacy
English Reading Game
Build a literacy-focused application that helps young learners improve reading fluency, comprehension, and vocabulary through interactive storytelling or challenge-based gameplay.

We're looking for: educational rigor blended with fun, narrative-driven design.
